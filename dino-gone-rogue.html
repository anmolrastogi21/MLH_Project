📖 HOW TO PLAY DINO GONE ROGUE

DINO GONE ROGUE is an endless-runner evolving into an AI-assisted boss encounter powered by your local Ollama Gemma model.

Phase Progression:

  • 1. RUN (36s): Sprint across cyber ruins. Jump over ground crates (SPACE or ↑) and duck under airborne recon drones (↓). Survive stumbles!
  • 2. WARNING: Siren sounds as a rogue cyber dinosaur arrives! Local Gemma generates its unique personality, backstory, and signature attack style.
  • 3. FLEE: Outrun the titan while dodging incoming ground quakes and laser hazards.
  • 4. CHARGE: Collect floating energy cells to charge your Rogue Blaster to 100%.
  • 5. FIGHT: Boss battle! Attack with Blaster (J / Space), unleash Charged Mega Bursts (K), and dodge telegraphed boss attacks!

Gemma AI Integration:

Your locally downloaded Gemma model dynamically generates contextual story snippets, boss profiles, tactical attack suggestions, funny commentary, and difficulty balancing. If Ollama is offline, intelligent fallback content seamlessly engages.

Lives & Combat:

You have 3 lives in runner mode (or 1 life in One-Hit Rogue mode). In combat, watch both your HP bar and the boss's telegraphed danger zones!
